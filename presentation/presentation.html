Introduction & Industry Domain Background

Domain Overview
🏪 Stationery Retail Ecosystem
Stationery retail stores operate on tight margins with high volume across hundreds of diverse SKUs—including ballpoint pens, gel pens, ruled notebooks, binders, highlighters, staplers, and adhesives. Items are purchased by students, educators, and enterprise offices on daily demand cycles.
📦 Operational Realities
Unlike grocery or apparel stores, stationery products have small physical footprints and low unit costs (₹10 to ₹100). Misplacement, damaged items, and unrecorded bulk shrinkage easily wipe out store profitability without disciplined inventory control.
🎯 Purpose of this System

To introduce an affordable, transparent, and framework-free inventory and point-of-sale management system that small-to-medium stationery stores can deploy with zero licensing overhead.

Problem Statement & Real-World Inefficiencies

Core Pain Points
📝 Manual Register Bottlenecks
Traditional storekeepers record sales and incoming goods in paper notebooks. Handwritten entries lead to arithmetic errors and delayed stock verification.
📉 Exam-Season Stock-Outs
Back-to-school and exam periods bring sudden 5× demand spikes. Without automated low-stock warnings, storekeepers run out of pens and notebooks, losing immediate revenue.
⚠️ Untracked Shrinkage & Damage
Dried-up marker ink, torn notebook bindings, and broken staplers are discarded informally without audit entries, distorting physical stock balance.

Database Design: 5 Normalized Tables (3NF)

3NF Data Model
Table Primary Key Foreign Keys Integrity Rules & Constraints
categories `category_id` None `category_name` VARCHAR(100) UNIQUE NOT NULL
suppliers `supplier_id` None Stores vendor contact, phone, email, and warehouse address
products `product_id` `category_id`, `supplier_id` `sku` UNIQUE NOT NULL; `unit_price > 0`; `CHECK (quantity >= 0)`
stock_transactions `transaction_id` `product_id` ENUM: 'INWARD', 'OUTWARD', 'DAMAGED'; immutable audit trail
sales `sale_id` `product_id` `quantity_sold > 0`; auto timestamp; total price calculation

Slide 13 / 20

End-to-End User Journey / Workflow

Operational Workflow
1. Product Intake
Manager registers new stationery items with SKU, unit price, category, supplier, and safety reorder point.
2. Inward Delivery
Supplier shipment arrives; storekeeper records INWARD quantity (+50 units). Stock updates and status turns Green.
3. POS Customer Sale
Student purchases 5 pens; cashier bills the item; stock automatically decrements and an audit record logs.
4. Threshold Breach
When sales drop stock to $\le 10$ units, system immediately transitions badge to Amber `LOW STOCK`.
5. Reorder Formula
Dashboard computes exact suggested replenishment units: $(\text{Reorder Point} \times 2) - \text{Quantity}$.
6. 1-Click CSV Export
Store owner exports the purchase list to Excel/CSV and sends purchase orders to suppliers.
